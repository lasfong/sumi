## Verdict

`APPROVE` — PRO-10 (Market Data Provider Decision) satisfies all acceptance criteria (`PRO-PROV-01` through `PRO-PROV-06`, `PRO-G-01` through `PRO-G-10`). Architectural Decision Record `docs/ARCHITECTURE_DECISION_002_MARKET_DATA_PROVIDER.md` provides an exhaustive, evidence-backed evaluation spike across Vietnam market data providers (SSI FastConnect, DNSE Open API, TCBS, `vnstock`, commercial vendors, and CafeF baseline), establishing the Provider Boundary Adapter Architecture (`MarketDataProviderAdapter`), secret-handling security, corporate actions isolation, fail-closed offline resilience, and the PRO-11 delivery roadmap. ## Implementation & Quality Audit

1. **Candidate Licensing & Legal Compliance (`PRO-PROV-01`)**:
   - Comprehensive survey across official broker APIs (SSI, DNSE), unofficial endpoints (TCBS), community wrappers (`vnstock`), commercial feeds (Vietstock/FiinPro), and public file archives (CafeF).
   - Redistribution, personal desktop use rights, and attribution obligations documented in ADR-002 (Sections 2 and 3.1).
2. **Authentication & Secret Lifecycle Security (`PRO-PROV-02`)**:
   - Explicit secret management rules: local environment variables / encrypted local config storage, zero hardcoded credentials, frontend key masking, ephemeral in-memory JWT session token caching without disk persistence.
3. **Data Depth, Timeframes & Benchmark Coverage (`PRO-PROV-03`)**:
   - Evaluated Daily historical coverage (>5–15+ years), intraday capabilities, benchmark indices (`VNINDEX`, `VN30`), and rate limits across HOSE, HNX, UPCOM.
4. **Corporate Actions & Timezone Semantics (`PRO-PROV-04`)**:
   - Enforced strict physical separation between `unadjusted` and `adjusted` price series (`PRO-DATA-06`).
   - Standardized `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` UTC+7 timezone semantics; weekly candles remain authoritatively derived by Sumi's internal `WeeklyAggregator`.
5. **Resilience, Throttling & Offline Invariants (`PRO-PROV-05`)**:
   - Exponential backoff retry strategy, 429 rate limit adherence, user-triggered on-demand sync invariant (no background polling/telemetry), and 100% offline local SQLite parity.
6. **Provider Boundary Adapter Design & Verdict (`PRO-PROV-06`)**:
   - Formally approved integration under the `MarketDataProviderAdapter` abstract interface.
   - Core domain entities (`Candle`, `Symbol`, `IndicatorEngine`, `ReplayService`) remain 100% vendor-agnostic with zero external dependencies.
   - Detailed technical scope, API contracts, and UI flow specified for PRO-11.
