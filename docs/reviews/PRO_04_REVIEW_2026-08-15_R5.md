## R4 Disposition

### Resolved

1. **Bollinger `std` Parameter Translation**: `IndicatorEngine` and `StrategyIndicatorAdapter` translate public `std` to `lower_std` and `upper_std` for the underlying `pandas-ta` library, retaining `std` as the public released/persisted product parameter.
2. **Deterministic Column Formatting**: `IndicatorRenderRegistry.ts` implements `formatBollingerStd`, deterministically preserving decimal precision for fractional floats (e.g. `2.25` -> `2.25`) and `.0` for integral floats (e.g. `2.0` -> `2.0`).
3. **Fail-Closed Contract Tests**: Unit tests in `IndicatorRenderRegistry.test.ts` verify exact matching of `BBU/BBM/BBL_20_2.25_2.25` and prove that rounded (`BBU_20_2.2_2.2`), default (`BBU_20_2.0_2.0`), or mismatched columns fail closed.
4. **Authoritative Parity & Edge Coverage**: Backend tests in `test_indicators.py` and `test_indicator_parity_e2e.py` confirm non-default calculation parity and column naming.
5. **Deterministic Product UAT Assertion**: `scripts/product-uat.mjs` exercises non-default Bollinger Bands (`length=20`, `std=2.25`) and asserts exact equality against session-scoped backend calculation columns (`BBU_20_2.25_2.25`, `BBM_20_2.25_2.25`, `BBL_20_2.25_2.25`).
