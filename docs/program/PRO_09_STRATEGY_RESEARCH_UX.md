# PRO-09 — Strategy Research UX

## Outcome
Strategy comparison is typed, reproducible, bounded, and resistant to overfitting or invalid recommendations.

## Acceptance boundary
`PRO-STRAT-01`–`PRO-STRAT-08` plus PRO-BT regression; validated metadata controls, immutable versions, train/test ranges, cancellation, bounded sweeps, and invalid-metric exclusion.

## Dependencies / exit
Requires PRO-01 analytics trust and PRO-03 data provenance. Exit requires reproducibility fixtures, cancellation evidence, browser UAT, and full gates.
